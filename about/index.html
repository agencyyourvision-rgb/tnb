One of the largest model makers in the world

For more than 30 years we have been building exceptional scale models — thousands of them, shipped to clients all over the world.

We are proud to say that our company produces some of the best and most beautiful models in the world, in terms of build quality, technical accuracy, beauty and detail.

Our goal is to amaze our clients — and to amaze our clients' audiences — whether the model is displayed in the main hall of a headquarters, at an international trade show, in a museum or at an engineering university.

We work for real estate developers, architecture and engineering firms, shipyards, yacht designers, oil and gas companies, hotel groups, government entities, machine builders and museums.
