“Seven Seas”Superyacht by OCEANCO

1:50Scale
Y720 · 109 m / 357.7 ft motor yachtOriginal
2023Year
Oceanco (Netherlands)Client

Seven Seas is a 109-meter custom superyacht delivered by Oceanco in 2023, with exterior and interior styling by Sinot Yacht Architecture & Design and interiors in collaboration with Molly Isaksen.

Built at 1:50, the model details every deck level, tender bay and swim platform, presented on an illuminated base for trade-show impact.
