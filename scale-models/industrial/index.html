Industrial Plant Scale Models

Industrial Plant & Engineering Scale Models

Complex industrial systems can be difficult to understand from drawings, renders and conventional presentations alone.

My FREE Custom QuoteExplore

TNBuilt West converts engineering information into physical scale models that make industrial facilities, process layouts and machinery easier to understand.

We manufacture models of refineries, LNG facilities, treatment plants, factories and other complex industrial environments scaled to explain process flows and layouts.

  • Plant layout models from engineering CAD
  • Transparent buildings revealing process equipment
  • Ideal for approvals, training and investor relations
  • Durable construction for permanent display
