Master Planning Scale Models

City, Urban Development & Master Plan Scale Models

Large-format models of city districts, waterfronts, ports and islands, assembled on site when needed.

My FREE Custom QuoteExplore

Scale master planning models make urban development projects easier to understand by showing buildings, infrastructure, transportation, public spaces, landscape and surrounding context in one physical representation.

TNBuilt West manufactures city and master planning models for developers, governments, planning teams, architects and infrastructure stakeholders.

  • Modular construction
  • Removable sections
  • LED zoning
  • Interactive elements
  • Projection mapping
  • Transportation
  • On-site assembly

Technical specifications

Technical FeatureTNBuilt West Specification
Standard Scales1:500, 1:1000, 1:1500, 1:2000 custom ratios
Manufacturing Materials & EquipmentAcrylic, polyurethane resin, Durable PVC sheets, brass, steel. SLA/SLS 3D printing, 5-axis CNC machining
Lighting SystemsMicro LED, programmable fiber optics, remote-controlled lighting zones
AssemblyManual labor. Larger models are manufactured in separate parts to allow safe transportation.
Turnaround TimeFast-track manufacturing from 6 to 10 weeks (according to size, design complexity, additional features)
