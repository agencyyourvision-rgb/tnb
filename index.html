Engineering Precision Across Diverse Global Industries

A physical scale model converts complex blueprints into an immediate, universal visual language. Whether presenting a waterfront master plan to city planners or pitching a new superyacht concept to private buyers, our custom models provide uncompromised detail and spatial clarity.

Architectural & Master Planning Models

Applications
High-rise residential towers, luxury villas, commercial business districts, resort destinations, and urban waterfront developments.
Key features
Topographical land contours, custom resin landscaping, interior architectural lighting, realistic façade finishes, loose deck furniture, and interactive touchscreen displays.

Marine & Naval Scale Models

Applications
Luxury superyachts, commercial passenger liners, cruise ships, naval defense vessels, and offshore support workboats.
Key features
Hydrodynamic hull profiling, custom deck furniture, tinted privacy glazing, etched brass railing, custom stainless steel/brushed aluminum pedestals, and internal LED deck lighting.

Offshore Oil & Gas, Energy & Industrial Infrastructure

Applications
FPSO vessels, offshore drilling rigs, oil refineries, LNG gas plants, and water treatment facilities.
Key features
Color-coded piping systems, acid-etched copper walkway gratings, structural steel lattice work, dynamic lighting, and mechanical sectional cutaways.

Mechanical & Technical Cutaway Models

Applications
Gas turbines, industrial pumps, subsea ROV robotics, and onshore seismic vehicles.
Key features
Motorized internal mechanisms, cutaway viewports revealing internal fluid dynamics or gears, and precision-machined aluminum/copper internal components.

From CAD Blueprints to Finished Model: Our Step-by-Step Process

Our manufacturing process combines engineering, digital fabrication and skilled model making. Client CAD and 3D information is converted into manufacturing data and then transformed into individually produced components, assembled and finished by our production team.

  1. 01

    Project Review

    We review the project information, required scale, dimensions, presentation requirements, deadline and destination.

  2. 02

    CAD/CAM Preparation

    Engineering and 3D design information is converted into manufacturing files suitable for our production equipment.

  3. 03

    Component Manufacturing

    Individual components are manufactured using appropriate processes including CNC machining, laser cutting and 3D printing.

  4. 04

    Painting & Finishing

    Components are painted and finished according to the project's color schemes, references and presentation requirements.

  5. 05

    Manual Assembly

    Individual components are assembled manually to create the completed model. Electrical circuits, LED lighting and other features are integrated where required.

  6. 06

    Quality Control

    The completed model undergoes inspection and testing before packing. Progress photographs and videos can be shared throughout production, allowing the client to follow the project remotely.

  7. 07

    Final Approval & Delivery

    Final quality assessment can be performed remotely or at our manufacturing facilities before professional protective packaging and international shipment.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a scale model cost?

The cost depends on the model size, the number of units of the same design, the complexity of the original project, the delivery time and location, and special requirements such as mechanisms, movement, LED lighting or special materials. That is why every request receives a tailored technical and financial proposal, free of charge, within 48 hours.

How long does it take to build a model?

99% of our models are produced within 4 to 10 weeks, depending on size, complexity and the number of models. Where others take months, we take weeks.

How long does shipping take?

We export worldwide via DHL or UPS cargo, in custom protective crates. 99% of our models are delivered in less than one week, including customs clearance.

What materials do you use?

Mainly acrylic sheet of different thicknesses, durable PVC, ABS, acid-etched copper, plywood, aluminum and steel, combined with high-definition resin printing and automotive-grade paints.

What is the largest model you can build?

We build models of any type and any size. We have produced urban development models of 18 × 12 meters, including assembly at the exhibition venue.

Is my project information kept confidential?

Absolutely. We operate with strict NDA protocols and full discretion over all confidential information, engineering designs and specifications received from our clients, especially for pre-launch projects.

How do I follow the production of my model?

Every project starts with a detailed execution plan agreed with you. During production we share status updates, photos and videos of every stage. Final quality control is done in person at our factory, or remotely through detailed images of the model.
